# Ridgeline 20 Sleeping Bag

**SKU:** CO-SLP-R20
**Category:** Sleep systems
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15

## Overview

A 650-fill-power down mummy bag rated for shoulder-season alpine use.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Temperature rating (comfort) | -1 °C (30 °F) |
| Temperature rating (limit) | -7 °C (20 °F) |
| Fill | 650 FP responsibly sourced duck down |
| Fill weight | 570 g |
| Total weight | 1.13 kg (2 lb 8 oz) |
| Shell | 20D ripstop nylon, DWR treated |
| Lining | 20D nylon taffeta |
| Zip | Full-length YKK, left or right hand |
| Packed size | 20 x 36 cm |

> Temperature ratings follow the EN/ISO 23537 standard. The **comfort**
> rating is the appropriate number for most sleepers; the **limit**
> rating assumes a warm sleeper in a curled position.
