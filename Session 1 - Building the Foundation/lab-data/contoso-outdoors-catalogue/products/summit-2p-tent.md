# Summit 2P Tent

**SKU:** CO-TNT-S2P
**Category:** Shelters / Backpacking tents
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Document owner:** Product Marketing, Contoso Outdoors

## Overview

The Summit 2P is a two-person, three-season backpacking tent built for
alpine approaches where weight matters but weather is not negotiable.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Sleeping capacity | 2 people |
| Seasons | 3-season |
| Minimum trail weight | 1.82 kg (4 lb 0 oz) |
| Packed weight | 2.04 kg (4 lb 8 oz) |
| Floor area | 2.7 m² (29 sq ft) |
| Peak height | 102 cm (40 in) |
| Doors | 2 |
| Vestibules | 2 |
| Packed size | 46 x 15 cm |

## Waterproof rating

| Component | Hydrostatic head |
|---|---|
| **Rainfly** | **1,500 mm HH** |
| **Floor** | **5,000 mm HH** |

All seams are factory taped. The rainfly uses a silicone/PU-coated 20D
ripstop nylon; the floor is a 30D ripstop nylon with a heavier PU coating,
which is why its rating is higher than the fly.

> A higher floor rating matters more than a higher fly rating for most
> campers, because ground pressure from a knee or elbow forces water
> through a floor long before wind-driven rain penetrates a fly.

## Materials

- Rainfly: 20D ripstop nylon, silicone/PU coated
- Canopy: 15D nylon micromesh
- Floor: 30D ripstop nylon, PU coated
- Poles: DAC Featherlite NSL aluminium, 8.7 mm

## In the box
