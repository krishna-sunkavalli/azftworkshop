# Summit 4P Tent

**SKU:** CO-TNT-S4P
**Category:** Shelters / Backpacking tents
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15

## Overview

The four-person sibling of the Summit 2P, sharing its pole geometry and
fabric package but scaled for family or group use.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Sleeping capacity | 4 people |
| Seasons | 3-season |
| Minimum trail weight | 3.10 kg (6 lb 13 oz) |
| Packed weight | 3.40 kg (7 lb 8 oz) |
| Floor area | 5.0 m² (54 sq ft) |
| Peak height | 122 cm (48 in) |
| Doors | 2 |
| Vestibules | 2 |

## Waterproof rating

| Component | Hydrostatic head |
|---|---|
| Rainfly | 1,500 mm HH |
| Floor | 5,000 mm HH |

## Materials

- Rainfly: 20D ripstop nylon, silicone/PU coated
- Canopy: 15D nylon micromesh
- Floor: 30D ripstop nylon, PU coated
- Poles: DAC Featherlite NSL aluminium, 9.3 mm
