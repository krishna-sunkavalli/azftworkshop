# Footwear Care

**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** All Contoso Outdoors footwear

## After each walk

- Knock off mud and rinse with clean water.
- Remove insoles and loosen laces to let the interior dry.

## Drying

Dry at **room temperature**, away from radiators, stoves, and direct
sun. Heat shrinks leather, hardens adhesives, and delaminates soles.
Newspaper inside the boot speeds drying safely.

## Conditioning

Treat nubuck and leather uppers with a conditioner appropriate to the
material every few months, or whenever the leather looks dry and pale.
Over-conditioning softens a boot and reduces ankle support, so do not
overdo it.

## Re-proofing

Apply a spray suitable for mixed leather and textile uppers when water
stops beading.

## Storage

Store dry, at room temperature, laces loosened, out of direct sunlight.
Do not store boots in a sealed plastic bag.
