# Down Insulation Care

**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** Down sleeping bags and down apparel

## Storage

Store down **uncompressed** — in the large mesh sack supplied, or hung.
Long-term compression permanently damages loft, and lost loft is lost
warmth.

## Washing

Wash only when genuinely dirty; every wash costs a little loft.

1. Use a front-loading machine with no agitator.
2. Use a **down-specific** wash. Ordinary detergent strips the natural
   oils from the plumes and leaves them brittle.
3. Wash on a gentle, cold cycle.
4. Rinse **twice**. Residual detergent is a common cause of persistent
   clumping.

## Drying

1. Tumble dry on **low** heat.
2. Add three clean tennis balls or dryer balls to break up clumps.
3. Expect **2-4 hours**. Down must be bone dry in the core, or it will
   mildew from the inside.

Do not dry clean. Do not iron. Do not wring.

## Restoring loft in the field

Shake the item vigorously and turn it inside out for 10 minutes in dry
air before use.
