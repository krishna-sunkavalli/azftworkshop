# Tent Care and Re-proofing

**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** All Contoso Outdoors tents

## After every trip

1. Pitch or hang the tent until it is **completely dry** before storing.
   Storing a damp tent is the single most common cause of premature
   failure, because hydrolysis degrades the polyurethane coating.
2. Shake out grit. Do not brush a wet floor — grit dragged across wet
   coating scratches it.
3. Store loosely in a breathable bag, not compressed in its stuff sack.

## Cleaning

- Sponge with cold water and a technical fabric wash.
- **Never** machine wash, tumble dry, iron, or dry clean a tent.
- Never use household detergent or fabric softener: both strip the DWR
  and can attack seam tape.

## Re-proofing

Re-proof the rainfly when water stops beading and starts soaking in.

1. Clean and dry the fly.
2. Apply a spray-on or wash-in tent proofer to the **outer** surface.
3. Cure per the product instructions.

## Seam sealing

Factory seams are taped. If tape lifts after several seasons, apply a
seam sealer appropriate to the fabric coating (silicone sealer for
silicone-coated fabrics, PU sealer for PU-coated fabrics). Using the
wrong sealer will not adhere.

## UV

UV is the main enemy of lightweight tent fabric. Avoid leaving a tent
pitched in direct sun for days at a time; a fly can lose significant
tear strength in a single long, exposed season.
