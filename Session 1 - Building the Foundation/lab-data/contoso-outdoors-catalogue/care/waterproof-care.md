# Waterproof Fabric Care

**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** Waterproof shells and rainwear

## The most common misdiagnosis

A jacket that looks like it is "leaking" is usually **wetting out**, not
failing. When the outer face fabric saturates, breathability collapses
and body moisture condenses inside — which feels exactly like a leak.

The membrane is almost never the problem. The **DWR** is.

## Washing

1. Close all zips and fasten flaps.
2. Wash warm (30-40 °C) with a **technical** wash designed for
   waterproof garments.
3. Never use fabric softener or bleach.
4. Rinse twice.

## Renewing the DWR

1. Wash and rinse first — DWR will not bond to a dirty face fabric.
2. Apply a wash-in or spray-on DWR product.
3. **Activate with heat**: tumble dry on low for 20 minutes, or press
   with a warm iron under a cloth. Most DWR treatments do nothing until
   heat-activated, and skipping this step is why people conclude the
   product "did not work".

## When it really is the membrane

Persistent leaking at a specific point, especially along a seam, after
a correct wash and DWR renewal, is a warranty matter. See
[Warranty policy](../policies/warranty-policy.md).
