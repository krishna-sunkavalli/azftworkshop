# Returns and Exchanges Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Supersedes:** Returns policy dated 2023-01-01
**Document owner:** Customer Experience, Contoso Outdoors

## Standard return window

Contoso Outdoors accepts returns within **60 days of delivery** for a
full refund to the original payment method.

To be eligible, items must be:

- Unused and unworn, other than a reasonable indoor try-on
- In original packaging, with all tags attached
- Accompanied by proof of purchase

> The 60-day window applies to **all products**, including the Summit 2P
> Tent and all other tents, sleeping bags, apparel, packs, footwear and
> cooking equipment.

## Extended holiday window

Purchases made between **1 November and 24 December** may be returned
until **31 January** of the following year.

## Field-test returns

Footwear and packs may be returned within the 60-day window **even if
worn outdoors**, provided they are clean and undamaged. Fit problems
only reveal themselves under load, and we would rather you found out.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Fuel canisters and other hazardous goods
- Gift cards
- Items marked "final sale"
- Custom or personalised items

## How to return

1. Start a return in your account, or contact customer service.
2. Print the prepaid return label.
3. Drop the parcel at any carrier point.

Refunds are issued within **5-7 business days** of the returns centre
receiving the item.

## Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same 60-day window. If the replacement costs more,
the difference is charged; if less, the difference is refunded.

## Relationship to the warranty

The return window is not the warranty. A manufacturing defect discovered
after 60 days is a warranty matter — see
[Warranty policy](./warranty-policy.md).
