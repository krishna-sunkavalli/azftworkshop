# Repair Services

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## What we repair

- Tent pole replacement and shock-cord re-threading
- Fabric patches and tears
- Zip slider and full zip replacement
- Buckle and strap replacement
- Boot resoling on selected models

## Turnaround

Typically **10-15 business days** from receipt, plus shipping.

## Cost

Repairs covered by the [warranty](./warranty-policy.md) are free. Other
repairs are quoted before work starts; you may decline the quote and
have the item returned at no cost.

## Why we repair

A repaired product is a product that is not in landfill. We would
rather fix a tent twice than sell three.
