# Sizing Guide

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## Measuring your torso for a pack

Pack fit is set by **torso length**, not height.

1. Find the C7 vertebra — the bump at the base of your neck when you
   tip your head forward.
2. Find the top of your iliac crest — the shelf of your hip bones,
   level with the top of your hip bones at the sides.
3. Measure the distance along the curve of your spine.

| Torso length | Trailhead 45 setting |
|---|---|
| 43-46 cm | Short |
| 46-50 cm | Regular |
| 50-53 cm | Long |

## Footwear

Measure at the end of the day, when your feet are largest, wearing the
socks you will hike in. Allow a thumb's width in front of your longest
toe for descents.
