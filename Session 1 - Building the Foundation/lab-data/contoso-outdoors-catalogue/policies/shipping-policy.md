# Shipping and Delivery

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## Delivery options

| Service | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | Free over 75 USD, otherwise 6.95 USD |
| Express | 2 business days | 14.95 USD |
| Next day | Next business day if ordered before 14:00 | 24.95 USD |

## Order processing

Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are dispatched the same
day. Orders placed at weekends are dispatched the next business day.

## Hazardous goods

Fuel canisters cannot travel by air. Orders containing fuel ship by
ground only and cannot use Express or Next Day, and cannot be delivered
to some remote addresses.

## International

We ship to selected countries. Duties and taxes are the recipient's
responsibility.

## Tracking

Tracking is emailed at dispatch.
