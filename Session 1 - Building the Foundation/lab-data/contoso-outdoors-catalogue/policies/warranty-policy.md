# Warranty Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Document owner:** Customer Experience, Contoso Outdoors

## Coverage

Contoso Outdoors products carry a **limited lifetime warranty** against
defects in materials and workmanship, for the practical lifetime of the
product.

## What is covered

- Seam failure not caused by wear
- Zip and buckle failure under normal use
- Delamination of waterproof membranes
- Pole failure under normal loads
- Stitching failure

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from accident, misuse, neglect, or improper storage
- Damage from improper cleaning, including household detergent, fabric
  softener, bleach, or dry cleaning
- UV degradation from prolonged exposure
- Modifications or third-party repairs
- Products bought from unauthorised sellers

> Storing a tent wet is the most frequent cause of a **declined** claim.
> Hydrolysis of the coating is preventable damage, not a defect. See
> [Tent care](../care/tent-care.md).

## Making a claim

1. Submit a claim with proof of purchase and photographs.
2. Our team assesses within 5 business days.
3. If approved we repair, replace, or credit at our discretion.

## Repairs outside warranty

We offer paid repairs for damage that is not covered. See
[Repair services](./repair-services.md).
